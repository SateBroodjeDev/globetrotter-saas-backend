Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your workspace settings. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

When you sign up for Pro or Business, you get 14 days free. No credit card is charged during the trial. You can cancel any time before the trial ends.

What happens if my payment fails?

We'll send you an email with a link to update your payment method. Your account stays active for 3 days, after which it will be downgraded to the Starter plan if not resolved.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. Contact support@globetrotter.io and we'll process your refund within 5 business days.

Can I pay annually?

Absolutely! Toggle the billing switch above to see annual pricing — you'll save 20% compared to paying monthly.
